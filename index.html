Approach

How the work actually goes

No discovery theatre, no 60-page deck you never open again.

  1. A real conversation

    Free, no obligation. You describe what's frustrating you; we'll tell you honestly whether we can help and roughly what it would take. Sometimes the answer is that you don't need us.

  2. Look at the evidence

    Your numbers, your systems, your funnel — whatever the question is about. Opinions are cheap; we'd rather show you what your own data says before recommending anything.

  3. A plan with priorities

    Short, specific, and ordered by what moves the needle. Including what to ignore, which is usually the more valuable half.

  4. Build and hand over

    Execution, then documentation and training so your team can run it without us. A dependency you can't escape isn't a service, it's a trap.

Financial intelligence

Prism

A financial dashboard that takes almost any table of transactions — a bank export, a QuickBooks CSV, an Excel workbook — and turns it into a P&L, KPIs, forecasts and scenario models. Every headline figure opens to the lines behind it, so a number can be checked rather than believed.

  • Reads your existing spreadsheets, including cross-tab layouts
  • Projections and scenario planning from your own history
  • Findings and a suggested budget, computed — no guesswork, no AI invention
  • Runs entirely in the browser: your financials stay on your machine

About

M-Y Consulting is Milan and Yash.

We're a two-partner firm based in Tampa, Florida. Our work sits in an unusual overlap: between us we cover the commercial and back-office side of running a business, and we build software. That combination is the whole point — we can tell you what your numbers mean, and then build the thing that fixes it.

You work with the partners directly. There's no account manager layer and no team of juniors doing the work under someone else's name. Whoever you speak to is who does the work.

We'd rather tell a client something they don't want to hear than take money for work that won't help. That's a slower way to grow a consultancy and a much better way to keep clients.

Good fit if you're…

  • Running a business where the numbers live in spreadsheets nobody fully trusts
  • Growing, but unclear on which parts are actually profitable
  • Doing work by hand that should have been automated a year ago
  • Marketing inconsistently, and unsure what's working

Not a fit? We'll say so on the first call and point you somewhere better where we can.
